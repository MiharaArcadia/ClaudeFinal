🥗 Carby – Your Smart Nutrition Tracker

Logging meals has never been easier. With Carby you track your food via voice input, barcode scan, or text search — and always keep calories, protein, carbs, and fat in view.

🎙️ VOICE INPUT
Just say "chicken breast with rice" and Carby searches the database automatically. No typing, no scrolling.

📊 FULL MACRO TRACKING
Track daily:
• Calories (kcal)
• Protein (g)
• Carbohydrates (g)
• Fat (g)
• Fiber (g)

🔍 HUGE FOOD DATABASE
Over 3 million foods from the OpenFoodFacts database — from supermarket products to restaurant meals. Barcode scanner for instant scanning of packaged foods.

💧 WATER TRACKER
Stay on top of your daily hydration with the built-in water tracker.

🧮 PERSONAL CALORIE GOAL
Enter your age, weight, height, and activity level — Carby calculates your personal calorie needs (TDEE) using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula.

❤️ FAVORITES
Save your go-to meals for lightning-fast access.

📅 FOOD LOG
All meals chronologically, filterable by day, with nutrition summary.

🔒 PRIVACY
No ads. No subscription. No hidden costs.
Your nutrition data is yours — optional Firebase sync, 100% anonymous.

🌍 TWO LANGUAGES
German and English — automatically follows system language.
